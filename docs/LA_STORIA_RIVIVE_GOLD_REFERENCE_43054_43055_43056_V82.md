## DEFINITIVE formula, non-negotiable
1. Start with **authentic archival B/W motion** (never animation or pan-zoom of stills called real footage). Chosen action must be legible and interesting, preferably with a relatively large face, expressive gesture or vehicle; confirm year and archival record and reuse permission.
2. At a precise frame, **freeze the actual original shot**. Generate a genuine **image-to-image photorealistic high-definition still** from this exact frame, using neighboring film frames as identity/geometry references where helpful.
3. On frame-stop, **transition quickly to modern photographic sharpness and natural full color**: distinct eyes, skin pores, hair strands and expression where source supports them; convincing clothing, fabrics, historic street materials, sky and lighting. The visual jump must be unmistakably gorgeous. Mere DDColor recoloring, LUT, sepia, contrast, global sharpening, generic grayscale blending, native pixel upscaling or continuously colored film are **NOT enough**.
4. **Same people stay people**, same objects stay objects, same camera position/pose/silhouette. Never invent a face/person from a ladder, carriage, window, wheel or blur. Do not declare historically exact facial microfeatures impossible to verify from low-resolution source. If highly ambiguous, choose better source, frame or angle.
5. Hold the **AI photograph almost perfectly still**: slow preset median ~2s, rapid preset ~0.9s (tiny natural photographic drift tolerated) then hard cut to *another authentically moving* B/W scene, never mere crops of the same frame counted as independent shots.
6. The desired SOCIAL MUSEUM V76/V78 profile uses a **mix of rhythms**, usually 20–26s, **not a permanent rigid sequence**. V82 preset explicitly supports `SLOW_MUSEUM_43054` and `FAST_SOCIAL_43055_43056`; subject choice determines rhythm. For slow use 2s B/W+2s photo, fast use ~0.9s+0.9s. Optional mix can start with 1 quick hook followed by more spacious and slower snapshots. Prioritize 4–8 **genuinely distinct shots**, no filler/duplicate zoom.
7. On-screen title ALWAYS **elegant English place and year**, e.g. `PARIS · 1899`, `NEW YORK · 1903`, `VENICE · 1896`, `LONDON · 1903`, period as `PARIS · 1920s` if genuine. Use elegant restrained typography legible on vertical smartphone, not copying watermarks or source creator logos.
8. Original suitable music, reveal accents matched to each freeze; no voice by default; no "before/after" technical overlays, no "coming soon" outro, no long fade to black. Cover, 4 polished English-language descriptions, source/rights and image-generation disclosure in metadata, ZIP.
9. **Quality gate**: compare original source movie + exact frozen source frame + new image. Reject soft faces, painted faces, artificial skin, obvious face/object swaps, invented crowd, broken structure. No 78% globally blended B/W source luminance (V77 regression). **Artistic manual visual review** cannot be replaced by a global image correlation or automated count of color-photo intervals. A technical pass is not artistic approval.
10. **Every claimed improvement must be shown in an actual playable MP4** and optionally a before/after sheet. If failed quota, rendering or review, acknowledge failure. Never claim a file exists until verified. Offer direct GitHub Release playable MP4 + ZIP for Android, archive upload only if independently verified. No paid API/Vidu usage without approval.

## Review checklist for new video
- On-screen title English location/year accurate? 
- First 2s immediate historic motion and early realistic photo reveal?
- Color still clearly reconstructs visually modern photography, not just colorizing grain?
- Faces are naturally sharp at the source-supported scale, with correct count/pose?
- People/objects stable, no object↔person substitutions?
- Alternation source motion/still color matched at freeze?
- Different true shots, rhythmic pace 0.9–2.5s, thoughtful music?
- Full MP4 decoded, ZIP CRC checked, web download tested, not spuriously labeled approved?
