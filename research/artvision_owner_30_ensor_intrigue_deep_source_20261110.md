## Narration: what is grounded and what is interpretation
- Flemish collection identifies the central man in a top hat as the target of mockery; women around him gesture, point or grin. It explicitly poses, rather than answers, whether he is a philanderer or a cuckold.
- Ensor's masks are central to his satirical imagery; bright abrupt colour contrasts and ambiguous relationships are supported by the official Ensor thematic catalogue.
