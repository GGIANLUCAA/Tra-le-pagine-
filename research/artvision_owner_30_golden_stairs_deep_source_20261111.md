## Historical interpretation, avoid invented plot
The picture was exhibited in 1880 and is associated with the Aesthetic Movement. Tate's own shop text describes debate between a decorative composition and figures like spirits in a dream. It is NOT a documented literal narrative with named protagonists, destination, or specific tune. The 18 women descend in ivory/white draperies holding trumpets and violins; birds are above. Musical harmony and the repeated steps/figures are factual visual hooks; the Walter Pater aphorism is an interpretive context, not a quotation spoken by the painter. Sources: institutional links above.
