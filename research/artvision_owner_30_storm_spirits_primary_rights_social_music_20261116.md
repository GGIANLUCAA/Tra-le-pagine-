## Object identity — primary collection VERIFIED
- **De Morgan Foundation official collection**: https://www.demorgan.org.uk/collection/the-storm-spirits/
- Object number **P_EDM_0034**, artist Evelyn De Morgan, **1900**, **oil on canvas**. Signature reported by Foundation: EDeM / 1900.
- **Canvas H 1225 × W 1765 mm** (122.5 × 176.5 cm); **framed H 1540 × W 2080 × D 85 mm** (154 × 208 × 8.5 cm). The Foundation records framed dimensions but this does NOT independently document current frame design or room; **gallery placement/actual display not verified**.
- **Interpretation explicitly attributed to the Foundation**: the three female allegories embody Rain (left, yellow dress, pours grey water from inexhaustible vessel), Lightning (right, red wings and winged feet, throws bolts), and Thunderclouds (upper centre, dark blue, wings merge into clouds). A quiet central refuge interrupts the violent sea. The Foundation suggests the 1900 Boer War context may support a reading of turmoil and hope; it is **an interpretation**, not proof of the artist's precise intention.
- Official related preparatory object: https://www.demorgan.org.uk/collection/study-of-female-arm-for-the-storm-spirits/ (D_EDM_0154, pencil on paper, listed date 1875). **Do not assert this drawing was made in 1900** or infer a precise chronology; confirm dating with Foundation if important.
