## Primary museum identification and historical interpretation
**State Russian Museum** (Mikhailovsky Palace online collection), *Sadko*, Ilya Efimovich Repin, **1876**, **oil on canvas, 322.5 × 230 cm**. Primary page, directly checked: https://virtual.rusmuseumvrm.ru/mikh_palace/collection/russkoe_iskusstvo_vtoroy_polovini_xix_veka/sadko/index.php . - Provenance: museum says received in **1897** from the **Tsarskoye Selo Alexander Palace**.
- Commission: future Tsar **Alexander III**, then Grand Duke Alexander, according to museum.
- Literary source: Novgorod bylina; Sadko appears in an underwater kingdom.
- Museum's interpretation: exotic beauties personify various nations; Sadko chooses **Chernava**, the Russian girl. Treat as museum interpretation, not universal scholarly fact.
- Context: Repin painted the work while on his Imperial Academy fellowship in France; the museum associates the folklore subject with his experience away from home.
