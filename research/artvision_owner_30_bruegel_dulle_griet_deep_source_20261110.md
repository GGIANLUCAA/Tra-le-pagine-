## Verified narrative / provenance
- Collector Fritz Mayer van den Bergh acquired the panel at the Hammer collection auction in Cologne **5 October 1894**, for **488 Belgian francs**. It was catalogued as a fantastic landscape and attributed to Pieter Brueghel the Younger. He identified it with the Bruegel the Elder *Dulle Griet* described by Karel van Mander in 1604; do not imply every step in the pre-1894 provenance is known.
- The institution says original owner/commission and route to Sweden remain uncertain; Rudolf II is reported as owner around 1600.
- Restoration with IR/UV/X-ray/Ma-XRF exposed a blue-green halo in the sky, removed old overpaint, and clarified the signature/date as **1563** (formerly assumed 1561).
- Meaning is debated; museum warns against unsupported religious, political or social certainty.
