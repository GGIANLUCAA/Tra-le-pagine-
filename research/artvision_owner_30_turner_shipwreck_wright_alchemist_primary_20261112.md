## #53 — The Alchemist, Joseph Wright of Derby (1771, rielaborato 1795)

Identità: Joseph Wright of Derby, titolo esteso museale *The Alchymist, in Search of the Philosopher's Stone, discovers Phosphorus, and prays for the successful conclusion of his operation, as was the custom of the ancient chymical astrologers*. Fonte primaria https://derbymuseums.org/collection/joseph-wright-of-derby/ ; opuscolo ufficiale https://derbymuseums.org/static/c2e3f53fbdea6e723e719bf2687cfe34/Year_of_Wright_Fold_Out_Map_DIGITAL-002.pdf conferma 1771 **rielaborato e datato 1795**.
