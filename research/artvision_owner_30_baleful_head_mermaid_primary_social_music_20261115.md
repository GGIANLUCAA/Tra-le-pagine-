## #40 — The Baleful Head — Edward Burne-Jones

### Identity, primary record, story
- **Official museum source verified**: Staatsgalerie Stuttgart media guide, **Das Schreckenshaupt** (German title): https://mediaguide.staatsgalerie.de/sammlung/das-schreckenshaupt/menschen_mit_hoereinschraenkungen/
- The museum identifies **1885–1887**, **oil on canvas**, **150 × 130 cm**, collection **Staatsgalerie Stuttgart**, acquired with Lotto funding **1971**. A separate **accession/inventory number is not given on this checked museum page: NOT VERIFIED**. Do not substitute a guessed number.
- The painting is the concluding scene of Burne-Jones's **Perseus cycle**, yet the museum states it was the **first painting of the series he finished** — strong documented editorial surprise, not an invented anecdote.
- Perseus and Andromeda stand beside an **octagonal fountain** in a walled garden. Their **right hands join** over the water; **Medusa's severed head** is held between them, but the fatal face is seen in the fountain's reflection. The museum specifically notes **fallen ripe apples**, **a dead leaf in the fountain**, **closed eyes of Medusa**, and Andromeda's absorbed gaze at the reflection. These details permit accurate voice-shot synchrony.
