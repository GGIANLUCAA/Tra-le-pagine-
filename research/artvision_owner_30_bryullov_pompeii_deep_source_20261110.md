## Provenance and verified history
Museum records Alexander Demidov collection, then gift to Nicholas I, transferred by Nicholas I to Imperial Academy of Arts in 1836, later Imperial Hermitage, arrived Russian Museum in **1897**. The museum relates the subject to Vesuvius's eruption in 79 CE and Bryullov's study of excavated Pompeii and Pliny the Younger's letters to Tacitus. The museum's record dates conservation in **1995** and **2023–24**; do not use images of older condition as if current. Source: official object link above.
**Caution:** exact calendar date of eruption is historically disputed; museum/older accounts cite August 24, but narration should say 79 CE unless supported by specialist archaeology. Painting is a dramatic 19th-century interpretation, NOT archaeological footage.
