## New primary-source facts (verified page, not downloaded binaries)
- **De Morgan Foundation object:** https://www.demorgan.org.uk/collection/the-storm-spirits/ — **P_EDM_0034**, Evelyn De Morgan, **1900**, oil on canvas. **Unframed canvas 1225 × 1765 mm; framed dimensions 1540 × 2080 × 85 mm**. This framed measurement is a real institutional catalogue field, but does NOT prove the current frame is original or establish a museum-room installation.
- **Signed and dated:** Foundation transcription **“EDeM / 1900”**. This can anchor a specific narrated close-up, conditional on inspecting a real HD file and confirming that the signature is actually visible.
- Official interpretation: Rain in yellow pours from a vessel; Lightning at right has red wings and winged feet and hurls bolts; Thunderclouds is dark blue near the top. A relatively calm central patch of sea is explicitly described as a hopeful counterpoint. Boer War connection is an **interpretation offered by the Foundation**, not a documented statement of the painter's intent.
