# GIULIANO · Documentary V4 — quality gates (owner-approved direction)

## Must not release a mediocre MP4

1. Original moving footage of authentic Pit 1 shall start at second 0. The opening title overlays genuine footage, not a static cover.
2. 38+ distinct source-licensed authentic photo assets, 60+ intentional evidence-locked shots. If a photographed artefact cannot substantiate the narration, do not silently substitute a lookalike.
3. Each factual statement has a source; each shot has an archival URL, license, original image SHA and a voice interval. Replicas are explicitly labeled “RICOSTRUZIONE MODERNA · NON ORIGINALE”.
4. Never label modern excavation photos as actual 1974 documentary footage. Date text means the finding date, not photo timestamp.
5. One fresh Google Gacrux broadcast-documentary narration; no fallback to robot voices. ASR sentence match fraction at least 0.78, with absolute timeline alignment.
6. Real native movie footage is licensed and distinguished from gentle 2.5D-like camera motion on still photos. No AI hallucinated archaeological actors or discoveries.
7. Audibility: voice over documentary score, duck score under voice, H264/AAC, 1080x1920, full ffmpeg error-free decode, detailed QA JSON and a CRC-verified Android ZIP. No publication pending artistic review.

## Visual director manual review after first master

- First 3s wow; first 12s comprehensible if sound is off.
- Every image has the correct subject; especially pigment evidence vs replicas, actual bronze weapons vs armours, actual tomb mound vs museum signage.
- Readability of statistics cards at smartphone size; no overwhelming captions.
- No repetitive blur-backdrop slides; serious wide landscapes can use dual-panel proof crops with both views derived from the same original.
- Lower music if voice is masked; avoid random comedy or mock-epic sound effects.
- No claims of guaranteed virality: use A/B test with two honest openings and actual retention measurements once published.
